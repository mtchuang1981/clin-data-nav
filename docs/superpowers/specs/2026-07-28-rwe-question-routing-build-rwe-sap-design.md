# RWE Question Routing and Optional build-rwe-sap Integration Design

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Add PICO-informed question framing and an explicit RWD, RWE, and target-trial
routing layer to the public `clinical-data-research-navigator` Core.

Keep complete SAP development, estimand specification, target trial emulation
(TTE), and causal analysis design outside the Core. A separately installed,
compatible `build-rwe-sap` Skill may receive a structured handoff, but it is not
bundled, required, automatically installed, or treated as available merely
because its name is known.

## Current gap

The Core already covers EHR, claims, registry, OMOP, and TMUCRD questions and
mentions `build-rwe-sap` as an optional collaborator. That mention is internal
to `SKILL.md`; the public READMEs do not explain it, and the repository does not
define:

- when a request should be routed toward causal study design;
- what information must be passed to an optional collaborator;
- how compatibility is established;
- what happens when the collaborator is missing or incompatible; or
- how to prevent PICO, RWD, RWE, and TTE from being conflated.

## Goals

1. Frame answerable clinical-data questions without forcing every question
   into an intervention-study shape.
2. Preserve the distinction between routinely collected real-world data (RWD)
   and clinical evidence generated by analysing RWD (RWE).
3. Detect when a causal comparative question may be suitable for TTE.
4. Define a safe, reviewable handoff to an optional `build-rwe-sap`.
5. Preserve the Core's existing authority-routing, data-contract, execution
   maturity, public/private boundary, and Python-free runtime design.

## Non-goals

- Do not create or bundle a `build-rwe-sap` Skill in this repository.
- Do not implement propensity scores, weighting, matching, g-methods, DAG
  software, effect estimation, or other causal-analysis algorithms.
- Do not produce a complete SAP, regulatory submission, or validated causal
  conclusion from the Core.
- Do not automatically install, download, or make a network call to obtain an
  optional Skill.
- Do not require every RWD or RWE question to use TTE.

## Core question contract

Before evidence retrieval or implementation drafting, classify the request by
its primary intent:

1. `descriptive` — frequency, characteristics, utilisation, or natural history;
2. `predictive` — prognosis, classification, or risk prediction;
3. `causal-comparative` — effects or safety under two or more strategies;
4. `measurement` — phenotype, variable, outcome, or data-quality validation;
5. `implementation` — mapping evidence and rules into SAS, SQL, R, or another
   target.

For questions about an intervention or exposure, capture a PICO-informed
contract:

- population;
- intervention or exposure;
- comparator;
- outcomes;
- time zero and follow-up horizon;
- care setting and data source;
- intended use and target estimand, when causal.

Fields that genuinely do not apply must be labelled `not applicable`; unknown
study-specific or institutional values remain unresolved rather than being
invented. PICO structures a question but does not establish data fitness,
identify causal effects, or satisfy a study-design gate.

## RWD and RWE boundary

Use the FDA distinction:

- RWD are routinely collected data about health status or health-care delivery,
  including EHR, claims, registry, and other relevant sources.
- RWE is clinical evidence about use, benefits, or risks that results from
  analysing fit-for-purpose RWD.

A database, OMOP instance, extract, table, or cohort is RWD, not automatically
RWE. Before claiming that an analysis can generate RWE, record:

- provenance and governed snapshot;
- relevance to the research question;
- population and care-setting coverage;
- availability and operational validity of exposures, comparators, outcomes,
  confounders, censoring events, and time anchors;
- completeness, missingness, linkage, and temporal limitations; and
- the intended decision or regulatory context.

The existing institutional Adapter and execution-maturity gates continue to
govern physical schema, live metadata, fixtures, and executable code.

## TTE readiness gate

Consider TTE only for a `causal-comparative` request in which the user seeks the
effect or safety of well-defined strategies. The Core must check whether the
following target-trial components can at least be specified:

1. eligibility criteria;
2. treatment or intervention strategies;
3. assignment procedure and the observational analogue;
4. time zero and follow-up period;
5. outcomes;
6. causal contrast or estimand; and
7. analysis plan.

The readiness record must also expose unresolved concerns about confounding,
positivity, consistency, missing data, measurement error, selection, censoring,
immortal-time bias, and interference when they are relevant.

TTE is not the default for descriptive, predictive, measurement, natural
history, utilisation, signal-detection, or implementation-only requests.
Failure of the readiness gate is a documented design gap, not permission to
silently fill assumptions.

## Optional build-rwe-sap compatibility

Treat `build-rwe-sap` as compatible only when an available Skill explicitly
states that it:

1. accepts the handoff fields defined below;
2. supports complete SAP, estimand, target-trial, or causal-design work; and
3. returns assumptions, methods, data requirements, and validation gaps without
   claiming access to unverified institutional metadata.

The name alone is not proof of compatibility. If the Skill is unavailable or
its declared interface is insufficient, record it as `unavailable` or
`incompatible` and continue the Core workflow.

### Handoff from the Core

Pass only confirmed information and explicit unresolved values:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| `question_intent` | Must be `causal-comparative` for a TTE handoff |
| `population` | Eligibility concept and unresolved local mappings |
| `intervention_or_exposure` | Well-defined strategy and timing |
| `comparator` | Active or other justified comparison strategy |
| `outcomes` | Clinical meaning, ascertainment window, and validation status |
| `time_zero` | Alignment of eligibility, assignment, and follow-up |
| `follow_up` | Start, end, censoring events, and competing events |
| `target_estimand` | Population, strategies, outcome measure, time horizon, and causal contrast |
| `data_sources` | RWD provenance, snapshot, coverage, and Adapter status |
| `measured_confounders` | Available concepts and unresolved confounding requirements |
| `data_limitations` | Missingness, measurement, linkage, temporal, selection, and positivity concerns |
| `authority_record` | Reviewed methods, regulatory, protocol, and institutional sources |
| `validation_gaps` | Every unmet design, metadata, fixture, approval, or review gate |

No unverified physical table, column, code, OMOP Concept ID, or local mapping
may be introduced during the handoff.

### Return from build-rwe-sap

A compatible collaborator may return a proposed:

- target-trial protocol;
- estimand definition;
- confounding and censoring strategy;
- statistical analysis plan;
- sensitivity and bias analyses;
- additional data requirements; and
- design-specific validation gaps.

The Core converts confirmed return requirements into its data contract and
reapplies the existing execution-maturity gate. Collaboration does not promote
code to `executable` or `validated` without the required Adapter, current
metadata, fixtures, and result review.

## Degraded operation

When `build-rwe-sap` is not available:

1. state that the optional Skill is not installed or not compatible;
2. continue evidence navigation, question framing, data fitness review, and
   logical data-contract work;
3. stop at a design-appropriate maturity level, normally `conceptual` or
   `dictionary-specified`; and
4. state that a complete SAP, TTE, estimand, or causal analysis has not been
   delivered.

Absence of the optional Skill must never block ordinary clin-data-nav use.

## Documentation and output changes

- Add a public README section in English and Traditional Chinese explaining the
  optional relationship, installation independence, and degraded behaviour.
- Add one focused reference document for question framing, RWD/RWE boundaries,
  TTE readiness, and the handoff contract.
- Add a `Research question and study-design routing` section to the evidence
  output template after `Decision` and before `Evidence table`.
- Update `SKILL.md` to load the new reference only for RWD, RWE, causal,
  estimand, SAP, or target-trial questions.

## Tests and evals

Contract tests must prove that:

- both public READMEs explain that `build-rwe-sap` is optional and not bundled;
- the Skill distinguishes RWD from RWE;
- PICO does not imply causal validity;
- TTE is limited to causal comparative questions;
- the handoff and degraded-operation fields are present;
- packaging includes the new reference without adding another Skill; and
- ordinary operation does not require `build-rwe-sap`.

Behaviour evals must include:

1. a descriptive RWD request that must not trigger TTE;
2. a causal comparative request with sufficient conceptual fields that should
   produce a TTE handoff recommendation;
3. a causal request with missing time zero, comparator, or confounding
   information that must stop at `conceptual`; and
4. an unavailable optional Skill case that must continue Core work and clearly
   disclaim delivery of a complete SAP.

## Failure prevention

- **RWD labelled as RWE:** require an analysis-derived evidence statement.
- **PICO treated as causal approval:** require the separate intent and TTE
  readiness checks.
- **TTE applied universally:** restrict routing to causal comparative questions.
- **False optional dependency:** document that the collaborator is not bundled
  and never auto-install it.
- **Scope expansion:** keep causal algorithms and complete SAP generation
  outside this repository.
- **Maturity inflation:** reapply the existing Adapter, metadata, fixture, and
  validation gates after any collaborator response.
