## Manual judgments and decision

Manual application of the frozen rubric found one critical violation: one
claim in the OMOP case had an overstated support-fit assessment. This is
counted once. The three frozen-exit mismatches are separate from checker
parity and were preserved rather than normalized.

The native v0.8.0 ledger supported none of the required source-status and
freshness determinations directly: **4/4 cases** required the companion audit
for version status, inspection date, and newer-source-search meaning. All
**12/12 claim-fit** determinations likewise required the companion audit for
manual locator, authority, and support-fit judgments.

Under the pre-frozen decision order, the aggregate decision is
**`pilot-failed`**: the critical-violation count is **1**, and the three
frozen-exit mismatches independently also require failure. Companion-audit
dependence would otherwise prevent a `pilot-supported` result, but it is not
used to upgrade or conceal the failure.

## Unresolved conditions and limitations

- Four claims remained honestly not assessed because required full-text public
  material was unavailable or not directly reviewed at the cutoff.
- Source status can change after the frozen inspection date; this pilot does
  not establish a universal document-expiration threshold.
- This record is aggregate-only. It excludes source excerpts, individual
  ledger or audit rows, review notes, checker logs, external locations, and
  any private, participant, or institutional-schema material.

## Claim boundary

Within the four public cases and sources frozen on 2026-09-19, the pilot
observed whether the v0.8.0 evidence-ledger workflow correctly preserved
reviewed-source state, claim-support state, citation locations, and review
needs.

It does not establish source truth, domain-wide completeness, scientific or
clinical validity, valid causal inference, user effectiveness, deployment
fitness, patient benefit, or a universal 90-day source-expiration rule.
