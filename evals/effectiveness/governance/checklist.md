# Governance documentation readiness checklist

Use this public checklist to prepare references to externally governed
documentation. Do not put the referenced material or a completed real instance
in this repository.

| Control ID | External evidence that must exist | Responsible role | Prohibited action while undocumented |
|---|---|---|---|
| `study-owner-role` | Recorded ownership and escalation route for the study | Study owner | Do not submit, recruit, or delegate accountability. |
| `institutional-path-request` | Recorded request for the institution to determine the applicable review path | Institutional reviewer | Do not assume that review is unnecessary or complete. |
| `scope-risk-benefit` | Institution-ready scope, intended use, foreseeable risks, and anticipated benefits | Study owner | Do not describe the study as institutionally accepted. |
| `external-storage` | Approved external storage location and handling record | Data custodian | Do not place a real instance or study material in Git. |
| `access-minimization` | Role-based access list and least-access rationale | Privacy or security owner | Do not grant access beyond documented roles. |
| `retention-deletion` | Retention period, deletion trigger, and accountable role | Data custodian | Do not retain material without a documented rule. |
| `consent-material` | Institutional determination and controlled participant-facing material set, if required | Institutional reviewer | Do not contact or enroll anyone. |
| `recruitment-plan` | Institution-ready recruitment route, population boundary, and stop conditions | Study coordinator | Do not advertise, screen, or recruit. |
| `incident-response` | Incident reporting, containment, escalation, and recovery route | Privacy or security owner | Do not collect data without an incident route. |
| `environment-freeze` | Reproducible environment manifest and change-stop rule | Technical environment owner | Do not start a study session on an unrecorded environment. |
| `rater-readiness` | Rater roles, training evidence, calibration gate, and blinding boundary | Rating lead | Do not score human outputs before readiness is documented. |
| `task-pack-commitment-plan` | External custody, commitment timing, leakage response, and later verification plan | Task-pack custodian | Do not create or disclose the real task pack or nonce. |

A structurally complete checklist may be
`ready-for-institutional-review`; it is always
`not-authorized-to-recruit`. This checklist does not select or determine an
ethics pathway.
